# Surplus Star Ai

Act as a professional full-stack developer, UI/UX designer,

and AI application engineer.

Build a complete, functional, visually stunning website for

my project:

FoodWise AI — Intelligent Food Waste Reduction

PROJECT OBJECTIVE:

Create an intelligent food management platform that helps

college canteens, hostels, restaurants, and event organizers

estimate food demand, identify potential surplus, reduce

avoidable food waste, and track food reuse and redistribution.

IMPORTANT:

I want a complete WORKING WEBSITE, not just a landing page,

static design, or frontend mockup.

8. AI-ASSISTED DEMAND AND SURPLUS ESTIMATION

Build a working estimation feature.

Use these formulas:

Remaining Food = Quantity Prepared - Quantity Served

Estimated Surplus =

max(0, Quantity Prepared - Estimated Demand)

Surplus Percentage =

Estimated Surplus / Quantity Prepared × 100

Calculate the percentage only when the prepared quantity

is greater than zero.

Use configurable demonstration thresholds for low,

moderate, and high potential surplus. Clearly label them

as demo settings, not official food safety standards.

If sufficient historical records are available, estimate

demand using the average demand for the same food item.

If historical data is unavailable, request an explicit

demand estimate and explain the limitation.

For every estimate, display:

Food item

Estimated demand

Potential surplus quantity

Surplus percentage

Alert level

Explanation of the calculation

Suggested next action

The user should understand WHY an alert or estimate appears.

Do not claim that a trained machine-learning model exists

unless one has actually been implemented.

Do not present rule-based calculations as advanced AI.
